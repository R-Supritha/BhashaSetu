## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an inclusive, grounded, and deeply warm pedagogical platform built specifically for primary schools and multilingual vernacular classrooms (specifically Hindi/Devanagari, Santhali Ol Chiki, and regional phonetic bridges). The visual narrative eschews hyper-digital, neon, or sterile corporate SaaS tropes, anchoring itself instead in the tactile, human reality of regional learning environments: hand-painted vernacular classroom signages, sunlit chalkboards, terracotta courtyards, and woven craft textures.

The primary emotional tone is encouraging, secure, respectful, and crystal-clear. The target audience includes young primary learners (grades 1–5), multi-grade rural school educators, and cluster resource coordinators using low-to-mid-range tablets, interactive smart-boards, and shared desktop setups under varied lighting conditions. 

The aesthetic style is **Warm Tactile Educational**:
- High-contrast, welcoming surfaces evoking textured off-white sun-bleached paper and lime-washed school walls.
- Chalkboard and slate-inspired focal containers that convey deep focus, familiarity, and dignity without harshness.
- Generous, organic touch targets designed for small fingers or stylus input on budget hardware.
- Grounded geometric shapes with soft, approachable curvature, creating an inviting, non-threatening environment for first-generation digital learners.

## Colors

The color architecture is derived from physical learning materials and regional Indian school settings: marigold blossoms (*genda phool*), sun-baked terracotta, deep classroom slate boards (*takhti*), and natural unbleached parchment.

### Palette Construction
- **Primary (`#D96814` - Marigold Ochre / Warm Saffron):** Drives immediate pedagogical action, phoneme completion, interactive word-matching rewards, and primary interaction triggers. High contrast against both cream grounds and deep green containers.
- **Secondary (`#1F4E3B` - Deep Classroom Slate / Forest Chalkboard):** Provides instructional authority, lesson framing, listening exercise zones, and reading containers. Replaces harsh blacks with a rich, organic slate green that feels reassuringly educational.
- **Tertiary (`#2B5E8C` - Peacock Slate / Inkpot Blue):** Dedicated to grammatical bridges, multi-dialect translation hints, linguistic audio controls, and educator moderation panels.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - Base Background: `#FBF8EE` (Warm unbleached paper / Soft Khadi tint). Zero eye fatigue under glaring outdoor sunlight or uneven classroom fluorescent bulbs.
  - Surface Raised / Card Base: `#FFFFFF` with warm undertones.
  - Surface Slate Container: `#16382B` (Chalkboard surface for active letter tracing and character display).
- **Text & Contrast Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Typography: `#23211B` (Deep walnut slate, WCAG AAA compliant on `#FBF8EE` and `#FFFFFF`).
  - Secondary Instructional Text: `#595548`.
  - Chalkboard Text: `#FBF8EE` and `#F5C344` (Chalk ochre) on `#1F4E3B`.
- **Feedback & Pedagogy State Colors:**
  - Correct / Affirmation: `#286E3B` (Neem green).
  - Remediation / Try Again: `#BF4322` (Terracotta brick red, warm and encouraging, not punitive).
  - Phonetic Accent / Syllable Highlight: `#E8A217` (Turmeric yellow).

## Typography

Typography in this design system bears the primary weight of linguistic justice and vernacular legibility. The pairing uses **Noto Serif** for anchor titles, vernacular hero glyphs, and literary instruction, accompanied by **Noto Sans** for multi-dialect body text, conversational prompts, and UI controls. 

### Multi-Script & Vernacular Considerations
- **Comprehensive Script Coverage:** Noto Serif and Noto Sans natively render Devanagari (Hindi) and Latin phonetics smoothly, pairing seamlessly with Noto Sans Ol Chiki for Santhali instruction.
- **Vertical Metric Buffering:** Complex matras, upper vowel modifiers (*shirorekha* attachments, chandrabindu), and subscript conjuncts (*halant*, *ra-fars*) require generous vertical line-height. Line heights are calibrated at 1.45x–1.6x minimum to eliminate clipping during cross-script switching.
- **Distance Readability:** Because instructional tablets are often propped on collective low wooden desks (*chowkis*) or shared across 3–4 children, font sizes skew 2px larger than standard enterprise UI.
- **Vernacular Glyph Scale:** The dedicated `vernacular-glyph-lg` and `vernacular-glyph-sm` tiers provide generous tracking and expansive line boxes for letter-tracing drills, stroke-order animations, and phonetic comparison cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **Sturdy Responsive Fluid Grid** anchored in pedagogical predictability. Children and rural educators need layouts where control locations remain uniform and cognitive friction is minimized.

### Grid Framework
- **Mobile / Portrait Handheld (< 640px):** 4-column fluid grid, 16px margins, 12px gutters. Primary action controls are stacked along the thumb-reachable bottom zone.
- **Tablet / Landscape Classroom Device (640px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, 24px margins, 20px gutters. Dual-pane presentation: Lesson content / Visual story on the left 5 columns, interactive vernacular phonetic exercises on the right 3 columns.
- **Desktop / Smart Display (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, max-width bounded at 1280px to preserve focal cohesion when projected to classroom walls. 40px margins, 24px gutters.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Every layout element adheres to an 8px base rhythm (with 4px substeps for tight badge padding and chip alignment).
- Minimum touch targets for young students are strictly enforced at `touch-target-min` (48px / 3rem), preventing accidental mis-taps during handwriting or symbol drag-and-drop tasks.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation rejects floaty, blurred, hyper-ethereal drop shadows in favor of **Tactile Classroom Depth**. Surfaces mimic layered physical learning materials—sturdy chipboard, wooden flashcards, slate boards, and paper overlays.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Level 0 (Parchment Foundation):** The flat `#FBF8EE` canvas with an optional sub-perceptible 1px paper-grain stipple. Zero elevation.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Activity Panel):** Solid `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a tactile 1.5px border in `#E6DEC8` (Wheat Ochre tint). Shadow is crisp and physical: `0px 2px 0px 0px #DDD3B8` (giving a stamped card feel).
3. **Level 2 (Active Learning Card / Slate Plate):** Solid `#1F4E3B` (Classroom Slate) or `#FFFFFF`, offset with a physical push-shadow: `0px 4px 0px 0px #CABF9E`, providing tactile affordance that tells a child "this tile can be picked up or tapped."
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Vernacular Audio Overlays & Dialogues):** Bound with a distinct 2px stroke in `#4A4639` and supported by a warm ambient diffusion: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 33, 15, 0.16)`.
5. **Interactive Pressed State:** All interactive pedagogical elements depress physically on click/touch: `translateY(2px)` with shadow reducing to `0px 1px 0px 0px`. This delivers unambiguous feedback on low-cost touch screens without relying solely on sound.

## Shapes

The shape system corresponds to **Level 2 (Rounded)**:
- Base components (inputs, word tags, action chips) carry `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii.
- Medium components (flashcards, phonetic dialogue containers, reading text blocks) carry `1rem` (16px) corner radii (`rounded-lg`).
- High-level containers (slate chalkboards, bilingual story modules) carry `1.5rem` (24px) corner radii (`rounded-xl`).
- Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) are exclusively reserved for audio-play levers, pronoun pronunciation badges, and language-switching toggles.

The geometry balances friendly approachability with structural discipline: corners are curved enough to feel playful and protective, yet squared enough to honor traditional print media and school notebooks.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Tactile Action Triggers
- **Primary Action (Marigold Push-Button):** 
  - Background `#D96814`, label text `#FFFFFF` in `label-lg`, font weight 700.
  - Border: 2px solid `#B8550B`. Bottom push-lip: `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 #944205`.
  - On active press: `transform: translateY(2px)`, shadow reduces to 2px. Minimum height: 52px.
- **Secondary Action (Classroom Slate Button):**
  - Background `#1F4E3B`, label text `#FBF8EE`. Border: 2px solid `#143528`. Bottom push-lip: `0 4px 0 0 #0D231A`.
- **Audio Prompt Button (Vernacular Speaker Trigger):**
  - High-visibility circular or rounded-pill container in `#2B5E8C`, housing an ear or loudspeaker icon alongside clear script indications (e.g., "सुनें / Ańjom me").

### 2. Vernacular Learning Cards (Flash & Match Tiles)
- Built on `rounded-lg` with warm white (`#FFFFFF`) or Slate (`#1F4E3B`) backgrounds.
- Tactile 2px structural outline in `#E6DEC8`.
- Prominently showcases the script glyph at `vernacular-glyph-lg` scale in the vertical center, with corresponding pictorial cues below and transliteration chips on the bottom fringe.
- Success state: Border turns `#286E3B` with a gentle cream-olive fill (`#F0F7F2`).

### 3. Audio & Phonetic Chips
- Compact pills (`roundedness: 3`) used for syllable breakdowns, gender markers, and language switcher tokens.
- Inactive: `#F0EADA` background, `#4A4639` text, 1px `#D9CFB8` border.
- Active/Selected: `#D96814` background, `#FFFFFF` text, bold weight.
- Includes a dedicated speaker wave icon indicating audio pronunciation availability.

### 4. Input Fields (Handwriting & Text Bridges)
- Generous padding (16px horizontal, 14px vertical) to accommodate larger finger positioning and regional keyboard overlays.
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 2px solid `#D1C7AE`, text `#23211B` in `body-lg`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#1F4E3B` with a solid 3px warm marigold outer halo (`#D96814` at 30% opacity). Never rely on subtle browser-default blue rings.

### 5. Checkboxes, Radios & Syllable Selectors
- Sized at 28px × 28px for effortless child interaction.
- Frame: 2.5px solid `#595548` with a 6px corner radius (checkbox) or circular shape (radio).
- Checked State: Fill `#1F4E3B` with a bold `#FBF8EE` hand-drawn checkmark or marigold center pip.

### 6. Classroom Slate Board (Hero Pedagogical Container)
- Dedicated instructional container (`#16382B`) framed by a simulated warm wooden bead border (4px solid `#8C5933`).
- Houses blackboard-style chalk typography (`#FBF8EE`), visual tracing guidelines, and bilingual parallel text blocks.
- Yields a focal zone on screen that communicates: "Teacher or Guide is speaking here."

### 7. Dual-Script Progress Indicator
- Horizontal track in `#E6DEC8` (height: 12px, fully rounded).
- Active fill in `#D96814` featuring an advancing playful footprint or pencil checkpoint node, clearly marking bilingual lesson mastery without competitive stress.
